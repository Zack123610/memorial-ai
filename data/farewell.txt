My dearest family, if you are hearing these words, I have already gone ahead. Please do not let sorrow be the last thing you remember of me. Remember the Sunday dinners, the garden in late spring, and the way we laughed when the stories ran long. I am proud of each of you. Be kind to one another, forgive quickly, and keep the house full of music. I leave you my love and the wish that you live with courage and tenderness. When you miss me, speak my name aloud. I will be listening. This is my last gift to you.
